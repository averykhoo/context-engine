## Design review

affects: the owner, whose words in decisions must survive verbatim; the next session, which reads decisions through find, why and the index; every deployed repo (adhoc's import is CE-7); a plain-git human who edits decisions by hand
- reduced: a correction rewrites the typed words instead of adding to them | rung: reduce | control: `record correct` only inserts `[text]` after one unique anchor and logs where; G-D10 strips every logged insertion and refuses a record whose `corrected:` lines do not match its body
- reduced: superseding or relaxing writes one direction of the link and not the other | rung: reduce | control: `decision supersede` and `decision link --rel relaxes` validate both records, then write both under one lock
- reduced: the generated index goes stale after a hand edit or a by-hand decision | rung: reduce | control: every decision write regenerates it under its own lock; G-D6 compares it with a regeneration and names `decision index` as the remedy
- reduced: the importer drops or splits an entry's words | rung: reduce | control: all or nothing: every heading must parse and every id be new before a byte is written; each body is the entry's text verbatim, stamped; `--dry-run`; a trial on a copy of adhoc's log is recorded in docs/evidence
- residual: an imported record's actor and date are read from a free-text heading, so a misread attribution is stamped as fact, and its `session` is the importing session | rung: collective | why: the single-file log has no structured fields, so only a reader can tell a misread | control: the whole parenthetical is kept in `attribution:` when it says more than actor and date, `imported_from:` marks every imported record, and CE-7 reviews the import diff before cutover
- residual: a decision's `code` links (file::symbol) rot when code moves, and why() then answers from stale links | rung: collective | why: an existence guard over decision links is G-T3, not built (CE-14) | control: `decision link` refuses a file or symbol that does not exist at link time; G-T3 when CE-14 lands

A: every decision is an OKF markdown file; the index can be regenerated by hand from its runbook section; corrections read in place under ## Amendments
M: one generated index checked by byte equality; one `corrected:` line format, written and parsed in one module (okf)
