US-12 (amended 2026-10-15c). The owner triggers these by hand today and does not need them built yet. Two loops:
- **AAR, per session:** what worked well and what could be improved (FRAMEWORK §6.12 has only a first note; today it goes in the ledger `summary:`).
- **Periodic review, per project:** is the system working? Read the AARs and the ledger, and decide which recurring notes become a task or a framework change. In Deming's Plan-Do-Check-Act terms this is Check and Act, which the framework lacks (session 2026-10-15c review).

Both apply to this repo as a project and to the framework as a project.

To design: where AAR notes live (a ledger field or a kind of its own); the review's inputs (AAR notes, flow measures the ledger can give, such as sessions per closed task, the age of owner questions, the pause-to-close ratio and red CI after a green local gate); its output (tasks, decisions, a dated evidence doc); and how the owner starts one (a procedure in procedures.md with its by-hand step, P16).

## Traps
- A review that only produces prose is the gap this task is meant to close: each finding must end as a task, a decision or "no change", with an id.
