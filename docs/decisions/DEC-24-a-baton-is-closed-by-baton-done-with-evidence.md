The owner, 2026-10-10 (session 2026-10-10a), on who can close a baton. Session 2026-10-09f accepted an editing agent's rewrite of FRAMEWORK §5.5 rule 3: the old text let "the owning session's clean close, or the session that completes the work" close a baton; the new text closes one only by `baton done` with evidence of the step done, from any session. The agent recommended keeping the rewrite, since a clean close without evidence was a loophole.

> okay with batons

So: **a baton is closed by `baton done` with evidence, from any session**; the owning session's clean close no longer closes one. Housekeeping still expires a baton older than two sessions into a task.
