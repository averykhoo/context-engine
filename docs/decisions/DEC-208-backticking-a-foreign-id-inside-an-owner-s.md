When an owner's verbatim words name an id of another repo, putting that id in backticks is markup, not a change of the owner's words: the characters the owner said are all still there, in order. The write-time refusal's remedy offers it. Quoting the words as a blockquote, which G-D3 does not read either, is the alternative for a writer who would rather leave the words untouched.
